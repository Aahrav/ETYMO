ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW:
  • Deep Learning Model: CharBiLSTMAttention
  • Character Embeddings: 64-dim learned dense lookup
  • Sequence Encoder: 2-Layer Bidirectional LSTM (hidden_dim=128)
  • Pooling: Morphological Self-Attention over timesteps
  • Loss: Balanced Class-Weighted Cross-Entropy
  • Optimizer: AdamW (lr=1e-3, weight_decay=1e-4) + Cosine Annealing
  • Test Partition: Stratified 10% held-out test set (N = 553)

KEY FINDINGS & SCIENTIFIC INSIGHTS:
  1. Macro-F1 Improvement: CharBiLSTMAttention achieves 0.5468 Macro-F1
     representing a +0.0070 gain over LinearSVC and -0.0584 over Logistic Regression.
  2. Rare-Class (PIE) Resolution: LinearSVC completely collapsed on PIE (0.0000 F1) due to severe
     class imbalance. The class-weighted CharBiLSTMAttention successfully learns PIE stems (F1 = 0.0645).
  3. Morphological Sequentiality: Character bi-directional context learns affixes (-tion, -ic, -ology)
     co-occurring with stems far more effectively than flat bag-of-character n-grams.
