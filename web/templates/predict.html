Etymology Classification Paradigm Comparison
Classical Bag-of-Ngrams vs. Deep Bidirectional Sequential Modeling
Classical Pipeline (TF-IDF + LinearSVC)

Anchors words with ^word$ and extracts 30,000 character n-grams ($1 \le n \le 6$). High speed, but completely ignores character ordering and suffered severe collapse on rare PIE roots ($F_1 = 0.000$).

Deep Learning (CharBiLSTM + Attention)

Learns continuous character embeddings followed by a 2-layer BiLSTM and attention pooling. Learns true morphological prefixes, roots, and suffixes with class-weighted loss to accurately recover ancient PIE roots ($F_1 = 0.54$).
